Stripe Fee Formula & Invoicing Math

Forward Calculation (what you receive after Stripe takes its processing deduction):

Net Payout = Gross Charge − (Gross Charge × Rate) − Fixed Fee

Reverse Calculation (how much to bill so you net an exact dollar figure):

Gross Invoice = (Target Net + Fixed Fee) ÷ (1 − Applicable Combined Rate)

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Stripe charge per $100? ↓

On a standard US domestic transaction of $100, Stripe charges 2.9% ($2.90) + $0.30 = $3.20 in fees, leaving you with a net payout of $96.80.

Does Stripe charge for standard bank payouts? ↓

No. Standard automatic payouts to your connected local bank account are completely free. If you request an Instant Payout to a debit card, Stripe charges a 1.5% fee in the US and Australia, or 1% in most other supported regions.

How do I get the client to cover the Stripe processing fee? ↓

Switch to "I need to receive $Y" mode above. The tool applies the gross-up formula to calculate the exact invoice amount needed so Stripe's cut does not reduce your net earnings.

What are Stripe's secondary or dispute fees? ↓

Dispute fees vary by currency (typically $15 in the US/CA, £15 in the UK, and €20 in Ireland/Europe per dispute). Additional optional tools like Stripe Billing or advanced Invoicing carry separate usage-based pricing.
